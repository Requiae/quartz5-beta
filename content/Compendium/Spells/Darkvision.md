_2nd-level, Transmutation_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (either a pinch of dried carrot or an agate)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature to grant it the ability to see in the dark. For the duration, that creature has [[Vision and Light#Darkvision|darkvision]] out to a range of 60 feet.
