_8th-level, Abjuration_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 24 hours

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is immune to psychic damage, any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, divination spells, and the [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] condition. The spell even foils _[[Wish|wish]]_ spells and spells or effects of similar power used to affect the target's mind or to gain information about the target.
