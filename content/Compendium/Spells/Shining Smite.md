_2nd-level, Transmutation_

- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The next time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack during the spell's duration, your weapon shines with bright white light, and the attack deals an extra `2d6` radiant damage to the target and causes the target to be outlined in light. Until the spell ends, the target sheds [[Vision and Light#Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have advantage, and it can't benefit being [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]].

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3nd level or higher, the initial extra damage dealt by the attack increases by `1d6` for each slot level above 2nd.
