_2nd-level, Divination_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc and a small sprinkling of powdered silver)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, you see [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Ethereal creatures and objects appear ghostly and translucent.
