_9th-level, Evocation_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over the creature you touch. The target regains all its hit points. If the creature is [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]], [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]], or [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]], the condition ends. If the creature is [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], it can use its reaction to stand up. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.
