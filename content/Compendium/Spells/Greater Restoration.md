_5th-level, Abjuration_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth at least 100 gp, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You imbue a creature you touch with positive energy to undo a debilitating effect. You can reduce the target's [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]] level by one, or end one of the following effects on the target:

- One effect that [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]] the target
- One curse, including the target's attunement to a cursed magic item
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores
- One effect reducing the target's hit point maximum
