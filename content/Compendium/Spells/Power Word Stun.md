_8th-level, Enchantment_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You speak a word of power that can overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range, leaving it dumbfounded. If the target has 150 hit points or fewer, it is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]]. Otherwise, the spell has no effect.

The [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] target must make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, this stunning effect ends.
