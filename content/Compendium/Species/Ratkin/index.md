Ratkin are the world's youngest race. No one knows quite sure where they came from. Some believe they were created when a powerful magician corrupted an ordinary rats nest during the Collision, others think they came from some plane of animal-people-hybrids, yet others believe that they were uplifted by some god of animals. Nonetheless, the ratkin themselves live short enough lives with short enough reproductive generations that the people who knew the truth have long perished. Yet the ratkin themselves remain strongly divided about what their origin is.

## Ratkin Traits

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Age.** Ratkin age fast and die young relative to the other races. They reach adulthood around age 5, and rarely live past 30.

**Size.** Ratkin are of similar size and proportions to halflings, standing 2 to 3 feet tall. Your size is Small.

**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Scurry Away.** Whenever you are dealt damage, you may use your reaction to move half your movement speed. This movement does not trigger opportunity attacks. ^scurry-away

**Languages.** You can speak, read and write Common and Rattish. Rattish is a language of high squeaks and fast dental rhythms. It can also be understood by ordinary mice, rats and rabbits, though those animals can not form complicated sentence structures back.

## Ideological families.

The elders of different ratkin families are extremely divided about their species' origins. And while the ordinary ratkin might not have a strong opinion on the specifics, this ideological strife has undeniably impacted their upbringing and abilities.
