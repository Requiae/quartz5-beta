The followers of Deistic Exaltation believe that the Great Rat has empowered them to do great things. The specifics of some great plan for the ratkin to fulfil are still hotly debated, but it is clear that the Great Rat has prepared them for a future of strife and disaster. Fortunately for humanity, most ratkin agree that this terrible future should be prevented at all cost.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 2.

**Devout believers.** You gain proficiency in the [[Skills#Religion|Religion]] skill.

**Chosen of the Great Rat.** You gain resistance to [[Damage Types#Poison|poison damage]] and have advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]]. As a bonus action, you may have your next melee attack deal `1d4` extra [[Damage Types#Poison|poison damage]]. You can use this ability an amount of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain these uses when you finish a long rest.
