The believers in the Arcanist's Folly believe that some magician or cult surged powerful magic into a rats nest during the Collision, and that magic has given the rats humanoid intellect and posture. They still revere magic, despite generally being of the opinion that the arcanists that uplifted them were probably not people who should be followed or revered.

**Ability Score Increase.** Choose to have your Intelligence or your Charisma score increase by 2.

**Magical obsession.** You gain proficiency in the [[Skills#Arcana|Arcana]] skill. In addition, learn a cantrip from any spell list. Your casting ability for this cantrip is the ability score you chose for the previous feature.

**A history of arcane experimentation.** When you cast a spell as a reaction, you may use your _[[Compendium/Species/Ratkin/index#^scurry-away|Scurry Away]]_ feature as part of the same reaction.
