The Extraplanar Pilgrims believe they are descendants from a proud culture of knights and guards who protect their kin and world from evil predators. These brave rats stumbled through a portal created during the great collision and have pledged to be the same for this world. Doctrine isn't entirely clear on which races and species exactly count as kin and which races count as predators.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 1.

**Knights of another realm.** You gain proficiency in the [[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]] skill. You may ignore the [[Item Properties#Heavy|Heavy]] property on weapons.

**Protectors of the meek.** You may use the _[[Compendium/Species/Ratkin/index#^scurry-away|Scurry Away]]_ feature when a friendly creature within 30 feet is dealt damage. If you end your turn next to that creature, you may prevent half the damage dealt to it by taking it yourself instead. Until the end of your next turn, _[[Compendium/Species/Ratkin/index#^scurry-away|Scurry Away]]_ is renamed _"Scurry Towards."_
