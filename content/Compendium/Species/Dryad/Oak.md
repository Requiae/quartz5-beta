Oak dryad are thought of as the strongest of their species. Powerful and tall, they stand head and shoulders above other dryads.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Bark Skin.** Your bark is very tough. When you aren’t wearing armour, your AC is 12 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use your natural armour to determine your AC if the armour you wear would leave you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armour

**Sturdy as an Oak.** You are sturdy and rooted. You have advantage on saving throws and ability checks you make to avoid being pushed, pulled or knocked prone.
