Apple dryads, like other fruit bearing dryads, are regarded to be the most kind and caring.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Steward of the Wood.** You have proficiency in the [[Skills#Nature|Nature]] and [[Skills#Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] skills.

**Fruit of the Forest.** As a bonus action, you can create a magical fruit in your hand. The fruit has all the properties of a berry created by the _[[Goodberry|Goodberry]]_ spell. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, after which you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.
