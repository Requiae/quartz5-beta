Pine dryads can adapt to any environment. They aren't the tallest or most beautiful dryads, they are the most resilient.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Hardy.** You have proficiency in the [[Skills#Survival|Survival]] skill.

**Evergreen.** You have resistance to [[Damage Types#Cold|cold damage]].
