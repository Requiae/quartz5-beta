Willow dryads are said to be the most graceful and agile. They have long, flowing limbs and move with surprising grace.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Long Limbs.** Your movement speed increases by 5 feet.

**Constricting Vines.** You know the _[[Thorn Whip|thorn whip]]_ cantrip. At 3rd level, you learn the _[[Entangle|entangle]]_ spell and can cast it once using this trait, you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for both spells.
