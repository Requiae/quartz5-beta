Cherry dryads are considered to be among the most beautiful and alluring, with their breathtaking white flowers and flowing branches, they are truly a sight to behold.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Alluring.** You have proficiency in the [[Skills#Persuasion|Persuasion]] skill.

**Natural Charm.** You know the _[[Friends|Friends]]_ cantrip. At 3rd level, you learn the _[[Charm Person|Charm Person]]_ spell and can cast it once using this trait, you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for both spells.
