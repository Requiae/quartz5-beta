_Prerequisite: [[Compendium/Species/Dryad/index|Dryad]]_

You learn the magic of the primeval woods, which are revered and protected by your people. You learn one Druid cantrip of your choice. You also learn the _[[Longstrider 1|longstrider]]_ and _[[Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_ spells, each of which you can cast once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast these two spells in this way when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for all three spells.
