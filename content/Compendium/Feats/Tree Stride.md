_Prerequisite: [[Compendium/Species/Dryad/index|Dryad]]_

You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, up to a maximum of 20.
- Once on each of your turns, you can use 10 feet of your movement to step magically into one living tree within your reach and emerge from a second living tree within 60 feet of the first tree, appearing in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the second tree. Both trees must be large or bigger. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, after which you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.
