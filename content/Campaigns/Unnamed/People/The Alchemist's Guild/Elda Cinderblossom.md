Elda is a fruit bearing dryad infused with energies of the plane of fire. Her fruits, known as emberberries, are a prime ingredient for certain potions. Her tree stands in one of the gardens of the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]] where they are allowed to harvest her fruit in trade for protection and the occasional shovel of fertiliser.

## Backstory

Elda's story begins in a alchemist's garden touched by fire of the planar variety. She was among the last in a series of experiments done by the alchemist; a hawthorn dryad sapling planted in a place so hostile to unadapted plant life. Against expectations her sapling didn't only survive, but thrive, gorging itself on the planar energies.

Before the sapling was able to fully adapt however, the garden was raided by [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]]. The aforementioned alchemist had been showing increasingly shifty behaviour, and had at this point failed paying their guild dues multiple times. After the raid the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Guild]] claimed the garden rich in ingredients. They identified many common yet hardy species, and a few rare magical species, amongst which was Elda's sapling. She was determined to be a hawthorn sapling with an adaptation towards Ignis, and her fruit were dubbed emberberries because alchemists are nothing if not imaginative when it comes to naming things.

Eventually, after around 40 years after the raid, Elda's humanoid body first manifested, lazily draped over her branches, and was discovered by her mammalian tender, an elf by the name of [[Sorisana Herbalar]]. Sorisana, this not being her first encounter with a dryad, approached respectfully and made conversation, during which she gave the newly manifested dryad the name Elda Cinderblossom and promised that the dryad's fruit would not be harvested until a deal was struck between Elda and the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]].

Barely a month later, the two parties came to an agreement; [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]] would become her personal tender and be freely allowed to harvest her tree's fruit. Should the unfortunate need be, Elda would be allowed to pick Sorisana's successor from any recognised intermediate and above member from the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]] as long as they are willing, to whom the same deal would apply. In return Elda would gain free reign over the garden, which would remain owned and guarded by the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Guild]], plenty of fertiliser, and free access to the botany sections of the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Guild's]] libraries. Finally Elda would gain full citizen's rights guaranteed by the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Guild]]. The entire contract was carefully written to ensure Elda would follow both the letter and the spirit of the agreement.

Contrary to the [The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Guild's]] fears however, Elda proved to be eager to work with her tender not because she saw a person to exploit, but because she saw a peer in [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]]. In the following years the young dryad became fast friends with the elder elf, Elda officially joined the guild as a botanist and the contract was amended to waive her membership fees, and the two promoted each other's growth as botanists. They also ended up finding and adopting an [[Aien Herbalar|orphan]] they found while on an outing.

After 43 years of cooperation and friendship [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]] drew her last breath. In the last decade the two finished training [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana's]] successor [[Aien Herbalar]] after which she retired. Her last years were spend mostly in Elda's grove where the elf spend her time drinking tea with her friend, reading her favourite books, and looking at her proudest work, a beautifully tended hawthorn surrounded by many rare herbs. At behest of her friend's last wish, Elda personally buried [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]] between her roots. Elda did not show her face for almost a year afterwards.

While [[Aien Herbalar|Aien]] has proven to be a hard and respectful worker, has always been beyond gentle in tending Elda's tree, and Elda loves them dearly, Elda struggled to connect with them to the same level as she did with [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]]. Eventually [[Aien Herbalar|Aien]] encouraged her to go and seek other dryads that have adapted to planar energies, just like [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]] often suggested. They helped her arrange an extended sabbatical with the guild which they agreed to under the stipulation that she'd be assigned a [[Sally|guard]] from the [[The University/index|University]], a guard she's supposed to keep an eye on.

## Personality

Elda is kindhearted but prone to disproportionate responses to perceived injustice. She is quick to forgive or ignore those with little options and swift to trim those who regularly stifle others for avarice.

She tends to teasingly refer to her peer as mammals, which has gotten her in trouble more than once, usually due to a passerby not appreciating her humour.

Her main interests lie in botany what her former tender used to call the art of tea. She doesn't understand why most fauna seem to be infatuated with shiny things, to her the only useful minerals are those that improve soil quality. Elda, however, does understand the sentimental value objects can hold, perfectly exhibited by her careful storing of her friend's favourite book and tea pot, both enchanted to outlast her lifespan, near her tree.

Elda struggles with understanding emotions and interpersonal relationships, especially her own. Given her first 56 years of existence as a tree, and how plants reproduce in a manner completely unlike animals, she is completely lacking in animal instincts as well as being aromantic asexual. Add her fragmented understanding of family structures and you've got a recipe for behaviours seemingly contradictory to her own emotions and the occasional faux-pas. 

A clear example of her fucking up is her trying to replace [[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana]], a friend she has known since before she manifested a humanoid body, with [[Aien Herbalar|Aien]], their child who they have raised for 14 years. Obviously this didn't work as they filled very different roles emotionally, but in her mind they acted in the same role as tenders.

## Relationships

- _[[Sorisana Herbalar|Sorisana Herbalar.]]_ Sorisana used to be be Elda's tender, peer, and dear friend. While Elda herself would struggle to properly describe her relationship with Sorisana, it is best described as queerplatonic. Elda is still deeply affected by the lost of her partner.

- _[[Aien Herbalar|Aien Theren.]]_ Aien was an orphan found by Elda during on of her outings with Sorisana. After the two adopted the kid, they trained them to be Sorisana's successor. Due to to her inability to have maternal feelings, Elda views Aien as her equal and close friend. She still refers to Aien affectionately as 'sapling'.

## Game Features

**Botanical Researcher.** When you attempt to learn or recall botanical information, if you do not know that information, you often know where and from whom you can obtain it. Usually, this information comes from a library, conservatory, scriptorium, university, or a sage or other learned person or creature. Additionally, since you are part of the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]], they freely allow you access to their libraries. Your DM might rule that the knowledge you seek is secreted away in an almost inaccessible place, or that it simply cannot be found.

**[[Apple#^Fruit-of-the-Forest|Emberberries.]]** Your tree, and by extension you, grow emberberries as fruit. Emberberries are an uncommon fruit that can be used as an ingredient in [[Potion of Fire Breath|Potions of Fire Breath]], [[Potion of Fire Resistance|Potions of Fire Resistance]], and other potions related to fire. While the emberberries your tree grows are part of an ongoing deal between you and the [[The Alchemist's Guild/index\|Alchemist's Guild]], the berries you grow yourself can still be traded for coin, favours, or information if you can find the right buyer.
